01Contact

Get in touch

02Getting started

Add, sort and check off

  • Add a task: type in the box at the bottom and press Done. Tap the calendar button next to it to make the task due today, tomorrow or next week.
  • Pick a list: the row of lists at the top switches between All, Today and your own lists. A task you add goes into the list you're looking at.
  • Check it off: tap the circle. Tap a task's title to rename it.
  • More details: tap the ⓘ button to set a due date, reminder, repeat, notes and subtasks.
  • Swipe: swipe left to delete, or right to flag. After a delete, tap Undo in the banner that appears.
  • Your own order: choose ••• › Sort By › My Order, then drag tasks into place.
  • Lists: tap + at the end of the list row to add one. Press and hold a list to rename or delete it. ••• › Edit Lists changes list colors and order.
03Reminders

Notifications at the due time

In a task's details, turn on Due Date, then Remind Me. You'll get a notification at the due time. You can also add an early reminder, from 15 minutes to a week before. From the notification, press and hold to choose Mark Done or Snooze 1 Hour.

  • Not getting reminders? Check that notifications are allowed in Settings › Notifications › BB Tasks. In the app, ••• › Settings › Notification Settings takes you straight there.
  • Repeating tasks: when you check one off, the next one is added with its own reminder.
04Widgets & Siri

Check off tasks and add them without opening the app

  • Widgets: press and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, and search for BB Tasks. Tap the circle next to a task in the widget to check it off.
  • Siri: say "Add a task in BB Tasks", or "Add a task to Work in BB Tasks" to pick a list.
  • Shortcuts and the Action button: the Add Task action is in the Shortcuts app, and on iPhones with an Action button you can choose it there.
05Backups

Your tasks are backed up to your iCloud Drive

Whenever you leave the app after making changes, it saves a backup to Files › iCloud Drive › BB Tasks › Backups. You can also back up yourself with ••• › Backups › Back Up Now.

  • Restore: open ••• › Backups and tap a backup. What you have now is backed up first, so a restore can be undone.
  • New phone or reinstalled app: open ••• › Backups. Your backups from iCloud Drive are listed there. If one isn't, tap Restore from File… and pick it in the Files app.
  • Backups show "This Device Only"? iCloud Drive isn't available. Check that you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is turned on in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
06Common questions

Quick answers

Do I need an account? No. There's no sign-up and no server. Everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud Drive.

Do my tasks sync between my iPhone and iPad? Not yet. Each device keeps its own tasks. To copy your tasks to another device, restore your latest backup on it.

What does Today show? Tasks due today, overdue tasks, and the ones you finished today.

How do I search? Tap the magnifying glass. Search covers every list, including notes and subtasks.

How do I clear finished tasks? Tap Clear next to the Completed heading. You can also tap Hide to keep them out of sight.
